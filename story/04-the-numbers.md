# THE NUMBERS: a 60-second Roblox story

**Premise:** In this world, everyone has a number above their head. Nobody knows what the numbers mean. A boy discovers they're the order in which everyone will disappear.

**Hook line:** *"In this world, everyone has a number above their head."* Frame 1 is a close-up of a smiling face under a floating **2,018,442**.

## Structure

It uses the same skeleton as the reference reel and CRACKS: a world rule, a planted clue, a discovery, a personal twist, a sacrifice, then an ending that answers the opening line.

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook: the rule | 0:00 | A crowd, each person with a floating number |
| The world | 0:03 | Scientists stare at a whiteboard of question marks: "Nobody knows what it means." |
| **Plant 1** | 0:05 | "You can see everyone's number... except your own." Leo jumps and spins, trying to see his. |
| **Plant 2** | 0:09 | Leo's notebook of every number he's seen. It lists 17, 18, 19... then Ellie, **21**. Number 20 is missing. |
| **Plant 3** | 0:14 | The promise: *"You'll never go anywhere alone. I'll always go first."* |
| Inciting event | 0:19 | The mailman (17), then the bus driver (18), then the crossing guard (19) vanish, one per word |
| **The discovery** (your logline) | 0:24 | Red pen through 17, 18, 19: "They were disappearing in order." Then the pen circles Ellie, **21**. |
| **Twist** (with a pause) | 0:32 | Leo stays up holding her hand. Ellie: *"Leo? Why is your number twenty?"* His number appears for the first time, there's a 2-second silence with a heartbeat, then "He was next." |
| Sacrifice and callback | 0:42 | "Don't go!" / *"Hey. I promised. I always go first."* Plant 3 pays off, and he despawns. |
| Ellie's turn | 0:46 | She goes too, hugging his plush |
| Second twist | 0:49 | A white flash, and she spawns on a **Roblox spawn pad** on an empty baseplate. Leo is waiting. *"Told you. You'll never go anywhere alone."* |
| Thesis, flipped | 0:54 | The mailman, driver and guard wave from up ahead. "Now Ellie knows what the numbers mean. It's the order we leave in... so nobody ever arrives alone." |

**The staging rule that makes the twist fair:** the camera sees what Leo sees. Everyone's number is on screen except his. The audience never sees "20" until Ellie reads it out. The clue was there the whole time, in the gap in his notebook.

## Making it look like Roblox

Cut 1 of CRACKS read as generic blocky 3D. For this one I studied frames from the reference reel and rebuilt the character and world kit (`video/web/rbx/`) to match:

| Before (CRACKS) | Now (THE NUMBERS) |
|---|---|
| Rounded-cube heads with big cartoon faces | **The classic Roblox head**: a cylinder with rounded edges, built on a lathe, with a thin **face decal** of small black oval eyes and a thin smile line |
| Plain colored body boxes | Sharp **R6** blocks (2×2×1 torso, 1×2×1 limbs) wearing **clothing textures with baked shading**: hoodies with drawstrings and a pocket, suits with lapels and cuffs, uniforms with patches, denim jeans with seams, painted-on shoes |
| Box hair | Chunky, **glossy** hair accessories (swoop, pigtails, bob, cap, curls) |
| Flat colors | Roblox materials: **Grass, Concrete, Asphalt, Brick, siding, shingles, WoodPlanks**, and the **Baseplate** grid |
| Gradient sky | A deep blue Roblox sky with wispy clouds, plus **glow and a saturated grade** |
| Uppercase 3-word captions | The Roblox-story caption style: **one lowercase word at a time, upper third** |
| Text only | Roblox **bubble chat** over the speaker's head |
| Fade out | R6 poses (arms-up jump, stiff walk, hug), **despawn into sparkles**, and **spawn-in with a ForceField bubble** on a SpawnLocation |
| 50° lens | A **62° wide-angle** camera kept close, like the in-game camera |
