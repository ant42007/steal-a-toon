# CRACKS: a 60-second Roblox story

**Hook line:** *"In this world, every lie you tell cracks your face."*

It follows the same skeleton as the reference reel: a world rule, the one person who breaks it, the kid who hates them, the breaking point, a planted line that explains everything, the kid taking the "villain's" place, and a last line that restates the rule, flipped.

| Reel beat | This story |
|---|---|
| Rule + instant proof (buzz, +$10) | Rule + instant proof (a kid lies, **CRACK**) |
| World joke + status quo | Politicians wear masks. "So nobody lies." |
| "Everyone except Kai's dad" + list of crimes | "Nobody except Lily's mom": rent, the store... |
| Kid becomes the opposite; the notebook plant | "The liar's kid" swears never to lie; the plant is **"I already ate."** |
| Loss: "Dad, I—" / "GET OUT!" | Collapse: "One more crack and she won't wake up." / "Then STOP LYING!" |
| Notebook reveal, in two layers | The fridge is empty (logic), then "I already ate" echoes back (personal) |
| Kid does the forbidden thing to save Mom | Lily tells her **first lie** so Mom can eat (and won't crack again) |
| Finishes the cut-off sentence | Mom's "I alr—" is finished by Lily: "I already ate, Mom." |
| Thesis flips the hook | "The people with the most cracks are the ones who love you most." |

---

## Script

**1. TOWN** (sunny street, camera cranes down)
> **NARRATOR:** In this world, every lie you tell cracks your face.

**2. FRONT YARD** (push in on the kid)
> **DAD:** Did you brush your teeth?
> **KID:** Yep! *(**CRACK**: a crack splits his cheek)*

**3. TOWN SQUARE** (truck across the crowd to the podium)
> **NARRATOR:** Little lies, little cracks. Big lies? That's why every politician wears a mask. So in this world, nobody lies.

**4. APARTMENT HALLWAY** (start tight on Mom's face, arc out to the landlord)
> **NARRATOR:** Nobody except Lily's mom. Her face had more cracks than anyone in town.
> **MOM:** Rent's coming Friday, I promise! *(**CRACK**)*

**5. CORNER STORE** (dolly along the counter)
> **MOM:** I'll pay you back tomorrow. *(**CRACK**)*

**6. SCHOOL HALLWAY** (kids point and laugh; push in to Lily's face)
> **NARRATOR:** At school, they called Lily the liar's kid. So Lily swore she'd never tell a single lie.

**7. KITCHEN, DINNER** (slow orbit around the table, one full plate)
> **NARRATOR:** And every night, her mom said the same thing.
> **MOM:** Eat, baby. I already ate. *(a tiny tick, off screen)*

**8. HOSPITAL** (slow push in on the bed, heart monitor beeping)
> **NARRATOR:** Then one day, her mom collapsed.
> **DOCTOR:** One more crack, and she won't wake up.
> **LILY:** Then stop lying!
> *(Mom looks away and says nothing.)*

**9. DARK KITCHEN** (dolly into the open fridge)
> **NARRATOR:** That night, Lily opened the fridge. Empty. It had been empty for months.

**10. MOM'S FACE, EXTREME CLOSE-UP** (pull back as her cracks turn gold, one by one)
> **NARRATOR:** Every lie her mom ever told... was so Lily could eat.
> **MOM** *(echo)***:** I already ate.

**11. KITCHEN, ONE PLATE** (push in on Lily)
> **NARRATOR:** The next night, there was one plate.
> **MOM:** Eat, baby. I alr—
> **LILY:** I already ate, Mom. *(**GOLD CRACK**: her first)*

**12. NIGHT** (pull back out the window to the lit apartment building)
> **NARRATOR:** It was the first lie Lily ever told. Because in this world, the people with the most cracks... are the ones who love you most.
