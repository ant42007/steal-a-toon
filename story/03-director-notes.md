## Notes on cut 1

### 1. The hook drags. Cut it in half.
The first **15 seconds** explain a rule the viewer already understood at 0:05. After the kid's CRACK, the plaza scene spends 8 more seconds on the same idea. It's a joke ("politicians wear masks"), then a 1-second dead pause in "Big lies? ... That's why", then a recap ("so nobody lies"). Anyone scrolling has left by 0:10.
**Fix:** cut the plaza scene. Fold the status quo into the line that turns the story: *"Nobody lies. Except Lily's mom."* The hook goes from **15.0s → 6.1s**.

### 2. The twist needs a pause before it.
At 0:42.6 Lily screams "Then stop lying!", and 0.5s later the narrator is already saying "That night, Lily opened the fridge." Nobody gets to feel the line land. The reveal shows up before the audience is ready for it.
**Fix:** hold on Mom's silence as she turns her face away from Lily, with only the heart monitor beeping. **Kill the music** on the turn. Hard-cut to the dark kitchen. The fridge opens with **no narration**, just the hum, the door thunk and the light spilling out. Then: "Empty." There's about 3 seconds with no voice before the twist.
I also cut the narrator's "That night, Lily opened the fridge," because the picture already says it.

### 3. Open on a face, not a street.
0:00–0:02 is an aerial shot of a street. The weird image, a cracked face, doesn't appear until 0:03, and that's too late for a first frame.
**Fix:** reverse the crane. **Frame 1 is a tight close-up of a smiling man whose face is full of cracks**, then pull up and back to reveal a whole town of cracked people under "In this world..." It's still one camera move.

### 4. Dad's head is out of frame on his own line.
On "Did you brush your teeth?" (0:03.7–0:04.5) Dad is cut off at the eyes on the left edge.
**Fix:** start the push wider and higher so his face is in the shot, then push in to the kid.

### 5. You can't see Mom's new cracks.
At 0:22.0 and 0:24.3 we hear the CRACK and see the popup, but on a face that already has 12 cracks the new one gets lost.
**Fix:** the newest crack **flashes red-hot for half a second, then cools to black**. That applies to the kid's crack too.

### 6. The CRACK! popup carries over the cut.
At 0:25.0 the store's popup is still on screen over the school hallway.
**Fix:** popups and flashes end at the cut.

### 7. The milk carton covers Mom's mouth.
In the store (0:23–0:24.5) the carton in her grocery bag sits right over her mouth on "I'll pay you back tomorrow."
**Fix:** lower the bag.

### 8. The gold close-up blows out.
At 0:52–0:54.5 the face goes almost white, so the gold cracks and her closed eyes disappear right on the echo "I already ate." That's the emotional peak, and it's overexposed.
**Fix:** bring the key light and the glow down so the gold reads as **gold**.

### 9. The fridge interior clips to white.
**Fix:** bring the fridge light down so the empty shelves read as shelves. Their emptiness is the point.

### 10. The mix is 2 dB quiet.
Cut 1 sits at −16 LUFS, and Reels/TikTok normalize toward −14.
**Fix:** use two-pass loudness normalization to −14 LUFS, with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling.

### Keep
- The word-by-word captions. They're in sync, and the per-speaker colors work: pink for Mom, cyan for Lily, yellow for the narrator.
- The hospital two-shot, with Lily screaming in the foreground and Mom's cracked face behind her.
- The "I alr—" interruption. It's the reel's "Dad, I—" device, flipped: Mom's line gets cut off and Lily finishes it.
- The pull-back out through the kitchen window to the lit building at night.
